# Carom

A quiet court. Two paddles. One very changeable line of attack. Carom is a
browser paddle duel where every return is a chance to do more than keep the ball
alive. Take on the machine in Solo, or challenge someone beside you in local
Versus.

The opening serve gives you a moment to find your rhythm. Meet the ball in the
middle of your paddle for a clean return, or catch it near an edge to send it
across the court at an angle. Each paddle hit makes the rally faster. Soon,
getting into position is only half the problem.

Move as you strike and the ball takes your motion with it. Spin curves the
flight, turning a safe-looking return into a late adjustment. Two golden bars
sway and rotate in midfield: read the tilt, bank a shot, and let a rebound open
up the space your opponent just left.

First to eleven takes the match, but you need a two-point lead to finish it.
At ten-all, a patient return can matter as much as an ambitious curve. Take a
breath, find your angle, and make the next contact count.
